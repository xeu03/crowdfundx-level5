CrowdfundX

All-or-nothing crowdfunding with milestone payouts and instant refunds — fully on Stellar Soroban.

Anyone can fund anyone, verified on-chain.

Level 5 — Blue Belt · Stellar Builder Program · testnet: 51+ wallets · repo: github.com/xeu03/crowdfundx-level5
Problem

Creators in emerging markets can't get funded

5–12% platform fees

Legacy crowdfunding charges creators and backers at every step, on top of payment processing.

Days-long payouts

Even after a campaign succeeds, creators wait days for settlement — a lifetime when payroll is due.

No trust

Backers can't see where money goes after funding. All-or-nothing promises rely on a middleman's word.

Geographic exclusion

Bank-only rails shut out creators and backers in under-banked regions — exactly where funding matters most.

Global crowdfunding market: ~$1.3B transactions in 2024 alone (Statista) — dominated by a handful of centralized platforms.
Solution

Crowdfunding as a smart contract

Every campaign is its own Soroban contract with a locked vault, milestone payouts and guaranteed refunds. No middleman holds the money.

🎯 Milestone vault

Funds are released to the creator only as scheduled milestones — backers see every payout on-chain.

↩️ Instant refunds

Missed the goal? Anyone can close the campaign and every backer claims their full contribution back.

⚡ Near-zero fees

Stellar transaction costs are fractions of a cent — platform fee is 10 CFX flat, paid only by creators.

📡 Live everything

Contributions, payouts and refunds stream into the UI in seconds via Soroban events.

Live demo: crowdfundx-level4.netlify.app · contracts deployed on testnet

Architecture

Three contracts, one factory, live events

Wallet (Freighter)→ React dApp→ Soroban RPC→ Factory→ Campaign ×N↔ CFX Token

The factory deploys each campaign with a deterministic salt; campaigns pull CFX from backers into their vault, pay creators by milestone, and notify the factory via invoker-authorized callbacks. Events stream to the UI with cursor pagination.
